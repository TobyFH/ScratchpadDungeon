# Asset provenance and replacement licensing

- `fonts/PatrickHand-Regular.ttf`: Patrick Hand by Patrick Wagesreiter, SIL Open Font License 1.1. See `fonts/OFL.txt` and the original notice retained in the HTML template.
- Existing hand-drawn SVG icons, generated portraits, corpse sketches, tile doodles and paper textures were extracted from this repository. No separate artwork licence was supplied. They are labelled `UNSPECIFIED-project-art`; this is not a grant of third-party rights. Retained as placeholders.
- Temporary audio is generated locally by `tools/generate-audio.py` from elementary oscillators/noise, without samples or a paid service. The generated audio and synthesis score are dedicated to CC0-1.0 for replacement and redistribution. It is placeholder audio, not polished music.
- Replacements must record their own licence/provenance in `data/assets.json`. Original files are retained through `originalPath`; replacing an asset does not overwrite its provenance.
