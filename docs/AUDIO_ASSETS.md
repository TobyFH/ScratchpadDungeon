# Recorded effects

The five original WAV files supplied by the project owner are in
`ui/assets/audio/`. They are used as supplied, without re-encoding or changing
their pitch. No separate licence was supplied; do not assume a third-party
redistribution licence beyond this project's authorised use.

| Stable cue | Recording | Trigger |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `erase` | `SFX_Erase.wav` | A covered tile starts erasing, including beacon reveals |
| `poof` | `SFX_Poof.wav` | `smokePoof()`, including ambushes, summons and pursuer arrivals |
| `tick` | `SFX_SlotClick.wav` | Each animated partial reel turn |
| `lock` | `SFX_SlotEnd.wav` | Each ordinary reel locks |
| `lockBig` | `SFX_SlotEndBig.wav` | A large single reel locks, including critical multipliers |

`ui/audio-samples.js` is the authoritative cue-to-recording mapping. Replace a
file at its existing path, or update its `src`, then run `npm run build:ui`.
The builder embeds WAV data URIs into the generated audio region in
`index.html`. Never hand-edit the generated bytes. `gain` trims the recording
before the SFX/master volume controls; `cooldown` coalesces simultaneous effects
to avoid a beacon or three concurrent reels clipping the mix.

The shared `GameSound` mixer decodes recordings once after the first interaction,
caches AudioBuffers, and creates a fresh one-shot source per playback. Recorded
and synthesised sounds share the twenty-voice limit, SFX bus, mute, master volume
and hidden-tab suspension. Music/ambience retain their own buses. Original
synthesised cues are fallbacks while decoding or if a browser rejects a file;
failed decodes are reported once in the console and in
`GameSound.stats.sampleErrors`. No delayed playback is queued for a past event.
Audio never consumes gameplay randomness or delays a game action.

Skipping animation omits the intermediate turn sounds but keeps the final lock.
Disabling critical visuals omits the critical reel and its lock, while retaining
the existing critical result cue. Uncovered travel does not play erasing.

Run `npm test` to check decoding, bounded playback, mute, offline operation and
event wiring. For listening, open the HTML, interact once, and use Audio options
to lower music/ambience. Reveal a tile, encounter an ambush, roll normally,
fast-forward a roll, and land a critical. Verify the five sounds, normal/large
lock distinction and SFX slider. Browser autoplay policies require the initial
interaction; a muted browser tab/system mixer can still silence the game.
